## 🔭 Currently building: [Helios](https://github.com/rossmfadams/solar-agent)
Helios is an AI agent for early-stage site diligence on utility-scale solar projects in New York State. You give it an address and, in under 90 seconds, it writes a cited viability memo with a 0–100 Viability Score and an interactive map. The memo covers:

- Interconnection: proximity to transmission lines and substations (HIFLD), plus congestion in the NYISO queue
- Environmental constraints: flood zones (FEMA), wetlands (NWI), protected lands (PAD-US), and parcel slope (USGS 3DEP)
- Permitting: the town's solar zoning ordinance and whether a moratorium is in place

Built with FastAPI, PostgreSQL/PostGIS, LangGraph and Claude.
